How to read the signals

Each market uses only the cycles that passed our backtest for that market (each forecast built from earlier data only, then checked against what happened). The more of them agree, the stronger the signal. Cycles give timing and odds, not guarantees or price targets.

SignalWhenWhat it suggests

How much more miners move

Mining stocks move more than the metal, up and down, because a mine's costs stay roughly fixed while the gold price changes its profit. In the latest fall, gold dropped 18% from its February 2026 high while AEM dropped 33% from its own high: about 1.8 times the move. The scenarios below use that ratio.

Miners also carry company risks the metal does not have: mine problems, rising costs, taxes and country risk. Over the last 10 years AEM moved about 2% and DRD about 2.7% for each 1% move in gold (chart below), so 1.8× is a cautious figure.

No Shariah-compliant silver miner was found, so silver is held through the metal only. Silver usually moves more than gold in both directions. The 10-year chart below compares gold, silver and the halal gold miners.

How to choose now

Gold's cycles give no reliable signal for the next 12 months, so there is no strong case that gold will rise. In that situation the metal is the steadier choice; mining stocks suit only a smaller part of the position, for someone who expects gold to recover. Check the Shariah status of any gold or silver fund before buying.
