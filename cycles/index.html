What is the presidential (4-year) cycle?

US stock markets tend to follow the 4-year term of the US president. The current term started in January 2025 and ends in January 2029:

YearNameWhat usually happensStatus
Year 1 · 2025Post-election yearNew policies; mixed, often a correction early in the yearDone
Year 2 · 2026Midterm yearCongressional (midterm) elections on 3 Nov 2026. Usually the weakest year, with the bottom around Sep–Oct, then a rally into year-endNow
Year 3 · 2027Pre-election yearHistorically the strongest year for US stocksNext
Year 4 · 2028Election yearUsually positive, with volatility around the November electionAhead
