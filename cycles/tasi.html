The Saudi stock market index, monthly since 1998: buy and sell signals from the cycles that passed our backtest. The presidential cycle is a US pattern, so its reliability for TASI is shown separately.

How to read the signals

Each market uses only the cycles that passed our backtest for that market (each forecast built from earlier data only, then checked against what happened). The more of them agree, the stronger the signal. Cycles give timing and odds, not guarantees or price targets.

SignalWhenWhat it suggests

BuyMost cycles up and the period rose in 65%+ of past yearsBuy dips

CautionMost cycles point down, but the record is not strong enough to sellDon't add; protect profits
SellAll cycles down and the period fell in 65%+ of past yearsReduce or sell
