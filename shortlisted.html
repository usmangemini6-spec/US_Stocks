How a stock gets on this list

  1. SAlgo model suggests BUY, OVERWEIGHT or POTENTIAL TURNAROUND.
  2. Salman Score is 7 or more and the MEGA 6 Score is above 2.
  3. Business Analysis rating is OVERWEIGHT or BUY.
  4. ThirdParty Valuation is not a value trap: any of Undervalued, Fairly Valued or Overvalued counts; “Possible Value Trap, Think Twice” and “No Data” do not.
  5. Analysts rate it Buy or Outperform.
  6. Price Target Upside is 20% or more.
  7. 3-year projection of the ThirdParty value keeps rising (FY1 ≤ next 12 months ≤ FY2 ≤ FY3) and FY3 is above today's price.
  8. Score TTM (quality) is above 50.

A stock must pass all 8 checks to appear here. The list is refreshed every time the Screener data is updated.
