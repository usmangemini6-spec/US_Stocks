What is the presidential (4-year) cycle?

US stock markets tend to follow the 4-year term of the US president. The current term started in January 2025 and ends in January 2029:

How it works: the presidential cycle is the average monthly change in each month of the 4-year US presidential term; the within-year cycle is the average change in each calendar month. Reliability counts how often the projected direction matched the real month since 2005, each year using only earlier data. Data: monthly averages from public datasets (S&P 500 since 1950, gold since 1971, US CPI). Cycle views are historical patterns, not financial advice.
